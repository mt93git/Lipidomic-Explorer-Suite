# Illustrative reference-cell assets

The GLB, preview PNG, cinematic JPG and MP4 in this directory were generated in Blender for Gerardo Barcia's Team frontend. These project-authored reference assets are distributed under the adjacent MIT LICENSE.txt (copyright 2026 Gerardo Barcia for these assets), compatible with the repository MIT license. They are illustrative neutrophil anatomy, not a reconstruction from lipidomics or a kinetic simulation. No human image data are embedded.

The viewer's bundled Three.js MIT attribution remains in www/team/js/cell_viewer.js. Vendor licenses are retained under www/team/vendor/. These attributions do not grant any rights to third-party logos. Cohort measurements come from the shared prepared matrix and are displayed separately.

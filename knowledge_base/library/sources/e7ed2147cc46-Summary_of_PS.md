### Intracellular pathways
Diagram labels: Other phospholipases (e.g., ABHD16A) remove sn-2 acyl group; LPS (sn-1); Lysophosphatidylserine lipases; ABHD12 (brain); ABHD6 (liver, kidney); Glycerophosphoserine + Fatty acid

## Signalling and Functions of LPS

### Receptors for LPS
GPR34 (LPS1); P2Y10 (LPS2); GPR174 (LPS3)

Diagram labels: G protein; Ca2+ flux ↑; Immune cell activation

### Special case
1-(11Z-eicosenoyl)-glycero-3-phosphoserine

True agonist of TLR2/6 heterodimer

### Key biological actions

#### Proinflammatory
- Augments mast cell degranulation
- Mast cell-dependent anaphylactic shock

#### In injured/damaged cells
NADPH oxidase activation → LPS generation
Enhances clearance of neutrophils → Resolution of inflammation

#### Immunomodulatory
GPR174 mediates suppression of T-cell proliferation

LPS (sn-2, often polyunsaturated acyl chain)

## PHOSPHATIDYLTHREONINE (PT) AND OTHER AMINO ACID-CONTAINING PHOSPHOLIPIDS

## Structure: Phosphatidyl-L-threonine (PT)
Diagram labels: Fatty acyl chain; Phosphate; Threonine

### Lysophosphatidylthreonine (LPT)
(sn-1 form shown)

## Biosynthesis and Metabolism
- Synthesised from CDP-DAG and L-threonine by a phosphatidylthreonine synthase (in T. gondii), evolved from phosphatidylserine synthase.

Diagram labels: CDP-DAG; L-Threonine; PT synthase; Endoplasmic reticulum; Phosphatidylthreonine (PT)

- In mitochondria (in vitro): PT decarboxylated to phosphatidylisopropanolamine

## Distribution and Biological Roles

### In animals
- Very low levels in normal tissues (e.g., brain)
- Present in human blood as a pro-coagulant
- Elevated in coronary artery disease

### In protozoan parasites
- Major phospholipid of Toxoplasma gondii (20:1 and 20:4 acyl chains)
- Required for asexual reproduction and virulence
- Inhibition of PT synthase → dysregulated Ca2+ metabolism and reduced virulence
- Target for anti-toxoplasma therapy

### Also found in:
Eimeria falciformis (sporozoite stage)
Some bacteria (e.g., Bdellovibrio bacteriovorus)

### Lysophosphatidylthreonine (LPT)
With a fatty acid in sn-1 only; acts in similar ways to LPS in vitro (exact in vivo role unknown).

## OTHER AMINO ACID-LINKED PHOSPHOLIPIDS

### Representative structures
Phosphatidylserylglutamate (PSGE)

Phosphatidyl-O-[N-(2-hydroxyethyl)glycine] (from brown algae)

### Other examples and notes
- E. coli uses PS synthase to make phosphatidylhomoserine (PHS), which can be decarboxylated to phosphatidylpropanolamine.
- Porphyromonas gingivalis and Bacteroides ovatus contain phosphatidylserylglycine (PSGly) and an acylated form (a lipopeptide).
- Ligand for TLR2 → may contribute to chronic periodontitis
- Phosphatidyl-L-aspartate and phosphatidyl-L-glutamate with carboxylate-phosphate anhydride bonds identified in rat brain.
- Phosphatidylethanolamineglutamate in Peredibacter starrii.

These and other complex lipoamino acids are more closely related to phosphatidylglycerol in structure and biosynthetic mechanism.

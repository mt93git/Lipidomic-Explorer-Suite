# BIS(MONOACYLGLYCERO)PHOSPHATE (BMP)

## (untitled panel)
A unique lysosomal phospholipid of animals

Bis(monoacylglycero)phosphate (BMP) is a minor but ubiquitous phospholipid of animal cells, highly enriched in lysosomal and late endosomal membranes and in exosomes. It has a unique stereochemistry in which the phosphodiester links only the sn-1 and sn-1′ positions of glycerol.

## 1. STRUCTURE AND OCCURRENCE
Stereochemical configuration: The phosphodiester moiety is linked to sn-1 and sn-1′ of glycerol, not to sn-3 and sn-3′ as in other glycerophospholipids.

## Localization and function of lysosomes
Diagram labels: Uptake at cell surface; Early endosome; Late endosome (multivesicular body); Lysosome (pH 4.6–5.0); Degradation of materials; Receptor recycling

- BMP is highly enriched in lysosomal and late endosomal membranes.
- Up to 15% of lysosomal phospholipids (70% in some macrophage/microglial cell lines; ~11% of total cellular PLs).
- Marker lipid of lysosomes.
- Absent from plants.
- Reported in some bacteria, but stereochemistry uncertain.

## Occurrence in plasma
Found at trace levels; ~40% associated with lipoprotein fractions and ~60% in the lipoprotein-deficient compartment.

## Key features
- Ubiquitous in animal tissues (~1–2% of total phospholipids).
- Distinct fatty acid compositions depending on tissue, cell type or organelle.
- Enriched in exosomes.
- Only lipid (of >1200 studied) that accumulated in tissues of mice and humans during aging.

## Proposed functions
- Structural component of lysosomal/late endosomal membranes; influences membrane curvature and dynamics.
- May regulate lysosomal enzyme function and intraluminal vesicle (ILV) formation.
- Involved in lipid trafficking and exosome biogenesis.
- Potential signalling roles (BMP can be hydrolysed to lysophosphatidic acid).

## Physicochemical properties
- Unique geometry gives BMP distinct packing in membranes.
- S,S-(2,2′-diacyl)-BMP is more tightly packed but less thermodynamically stable than S,S-(3,3′-diacyl)-BMP.
- High content of unsaturated long-chain fatty acids confers high fluidity and curvature-favouring properties.

## Biosynthetic origin (proposed)
- Derived from lysophosphatidic acid (LPA): acylation → monoacylglycerol-3-phosphate (MG3P) → condensation of two MG3P molecules → BMP + H2O + Pi.
- Enzyme(s) and exact fatty acid positions (sn-2/2′ vs sn-3/3′) remain to be established.

## Metabolism
- Hydrolysed by specific phospholipase A1-like enzyme → lysophosphatidic acid (LPA).
- Turnover is slow; enriched in long-lived membranes such as lysosomes.

## Significance
- Biomarker of lysosomes and indicator of endo-lysosomal function.
- Alterations may be involved in lysosomal storage disorders, neurodegeneration and inflammatory diseases (under study).

## Bacterial occurrence
- Agrobacterium tumefaciens can convert lyso-phosphatidylglycerol to two isoforms of BMP.
- Reported in some alkalophilic Bacillus spp.
- Whether bacterial BMPs have the unique animal stereochemistry remains unproven.

## Conclusions
- BMP is a unique stereochemical phospholipid of animals, concentrated in lysosomes and exosomes.
- Distinct compositions suggest specialized membrane roles and functions.
- Key aspects of its biosynthesis and function are active areas of research.

# 4. CATABOLISM OF PHOSPHATIDYLCHOLINE (PC)

## (untitled panel)
Phosphatidylcholine in membranes is degraded by phospholipases that act at different bonds of the molecule. The products are recycled for re-synthesis, used in signalling pathways, or serve as precursors for other metabolites. Most of the released choline is returned to the liver (CDP-choline cycle) for reuse in PC biosynthesis.

## C. Fate of catabolic products
## Choline utilisation
- Re-used for PC biosynthesis (via CDP-choline cycle)
- Oxidized to betaine (kidney, liver)
- Donor of methyl groups for S-adenosylmethionine
- Precursor of acetylcholine in nervous tissues

## Signalling roles
- DAG → activates PKC and other kinases
- Phosphatidic acid (PA) → mTOR, cell growth
- Lysophosphatidylcholine (LPC) → inflammatory and other pathways

## D. Catabolism of PC in plants and microorganisms
## Plants
- Non-specific phospholipase Cs (NPCs): 6 isoforms in Arabidopsis.
- Broad substrate specificity → generate diacylglycerols from many phospholipids.
- NPC4s hydrolyse glycosylinositol phosphorylceramide and galactosyldiacylglycerols.
- Phospholipase D (PLD) is especially important in plant signalling.

Diagram labels: PC; NPCs/PLD; DAG + PCo; signalling

## Yeasts and fungi
- Kennedy pathway + remodelling enzymes (Gpc1, Ale1, etc.).
- Remodelling changes fatty acid composition.
- Important for growth and survival.

## Bacteria
Some Gram-positive bacteria possess PLD and NPC-like PLCs that catabolize PC.

## Major phospholipases acting on PC in animals
| Enzyme family (no. isoform families) | Bond cleaved | Main products | Roles |
|---|---|---|---|
| PLA2 (≥15) | sn-2 ester | LPC + free fatty acid | Lipid remodelling, eicosanoid precursors, signalling |
| PLC (6) | Phosphodiester | DAG + phosphocholine | Signalling (PKC activation) |
| PLD (3) | Phosphodiester | PA + choline | Signalling (mTOR, growth) |
| PLB (2) | Both ester bonds | Glycerophosphocholine + fatty acids | Complete degradation |

## Key points
- PC is rapidly hydrolysed by phospholipases A2, C, D and B.
- The products are recycled for resynthesis or act in signalling.
- Choline is reused (CDP-choline cycle) or converted to betaine or acetylcholine.
- PC from plasma lipoproteins is taken up by the liver and converted to triacylglycerols.
- In plants and microbes, non-specific PLCs and PLDs generate DAGs for membrane dynamics and stress responses.

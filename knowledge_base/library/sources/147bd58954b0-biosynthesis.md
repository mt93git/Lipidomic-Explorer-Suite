# 3. CHOLESTEROL BIOSYNTHESIS

## (untitled panel)
At least ~30 enzymatic reactions are required for cholesterol biosynthesis (Bloch & Lynen, Nobel Prize, 1964). The pathway described below is the classical mevalonate (MVA) pathway. Synthesis occurs mainly in liver, but also in brain, nervous system, skin and all nucleated cells. First, acetyl-CoA is converted to mevalonic acid in the cytosol. Subsequent reactions occur on the endoplasmic reticulum (ER). reductase is a key regulatory, rate-limiting enzyme and the target of statin drugs.

## 1. STEP 1. L-MEVALONIC ACID (cytosol)
Diagram labels: 2 Acetyl-CoA; Acetoacetyl-CoA; HMG-CoA synthase; 3-Hydroxy-3-methyl-glutaryl-CoA (HMG-CoA); NADPH + H+; NADP+; HMG-CoA reductase; L-Mevalonic acid (MVA)

## KEY CONTROL POINT
- Rate-limiting step in sterol synthesis
- Regulated at transcriptional and post-translational levels (phosphorylation/dephosphorylation)
- Inhibited by statin drugs

## 2. STEP 2. ACTIVATION OF MVA TO ISOPRENE UNITS (ER membrane)
Diagram labels: Mevalonate; Mevalonate kinase; Mevalonate-5-phosphate (MVA-5-P); Phospho-mevalonate kinase; Mevalonate-5-pyrophosphate (MVA-5-PP); Mevalonate-5-pyrophosphate decarboxylase; CO2; 5-Isopentenyl pyrophosphate (IPP); Isopentenyl diphosphate isomerase; 3,3-Dimethylallyl pyrophosphate (DMAPP)

## Key points
- Two phosphorylation steps activate MVA.
- Decarboxylation gives 5-isopentenyl pyrophosphate (IPP).
- IPP is in equilibrium with its electrophilic isomer 3,3-dimethylallyl pyrophosphate (DMAPP).
- IPP = universal C5 isoprene building block.

P = phosphate; OPP = pyrophosphate

## 3. STEP 3. FROM ISOPRENE UNITS TO SQUALENE (ER membrane)
Diagram labels: IPP (C5); DMAPP (C5); Geranyl pyrophosphate synthase; Geranyl pyrophosphate (GPP, C10); Farnesyl pyrophosphate synthase; Farnesyl pyrophosphate (FPP, C15); Squalene synthase (2 steps); Presqualene pyrophosphate (PSPP, C30); NADPH + H+; NADP+ + PPi; Squalene (C30)

## Key points
- Head-to-tail condensations of isoprene units build larger terpenoids.
- FPP is the branch point for sterol and non-sterol pathways.
- Squalene synthase is the first committed enzyme for sterol synthesis.

## 4. STEP 4. SQUALENE TO LANOSTEROL (ER membrane)
Diagram labels: Squalene (C30); Squalene monooxygenase (SQM); NADPH + O2; NADP+ + H2O; Squalene 2,3-epoxide; Squalene epoxide lanosterol-cyclase (oxidosqualene cyclase); Lanosterol

## Key points
- SQM inserts O at C-2,3 (rate-limiting step of committed pathway).
- Cyclase catalyses complex cyclisation with 1,2-methyl and hydride shifts.
- Sterol carrier protein-1 (SCP1), NADPH, FAD and O2 are required.

## 5. STEP 5. LANOSTEROL TO CHOLESTEROL (ER membrane)
Diagram labels: Lanosterol; ~19 reactions; Demethylations; Desaturations; Isomerizations; Reductions; Zymosterol; TWO LATE PATHWAYS TO CHOLESTEROL; Bloch pathway (major in most tissues); Kandutsch-Russell pathway (major in brain); 3 steps; Desmosterol (Δ24-sterol); Cholesterol (cholest-5-en-3β-ol); 7-Dehydrocholesterol (Δ7-sterol)

## Key points
- Lanosterol is converted to cholesterol in ~19 enzymatic steps.
- Bloch pathway uses desmosterol (Δ24-sterol) as immediate precursor.
- Kandutsch-Russell pathway uses 7-dehydrocholesterol (Δ7-sterol).
- Brain uses Kandutsch-Russell pathway predominantly.

## (untitled panel)
Overall: acetyl-CoA → MVA → IPP/DMAPP → FPP → squalene → lanosterol → cholesterol

## REGULATION OF THE PATHWAY
- HMG-CoA reductase is the major control point.
- Regulated by: SREBP transcription factors, feedback by sterols, insulin, glucagon, AMPK.
- Statins (e.g., atorvastatin, simvastatin) competitively inhibit HMG-CoA reductase.
- Squalene monooxygenase is another control point.
- Cholesterol regulates its own synthesis and cellular uptake via SREBP and SCAP.

## ENERGY COST (approximate)
To synthesise 1 molecule of cholesterol: 18 ATP (direct), 16 NADPH, 11 O2. acetyl-CoA → ~100 ATP equivalents.

## TRANSPORT AFTER SYNTHESIS
- Cholesterol is exported from liver in VLDL → LDL.
- Peripheral tissues obtain cholesterol from LDL receptors or by synthesis de novo.
- Dietary cholesterol (eggs, red meat) is absorbed in intestines and enters circulation.

## ALTERNATIVE BIOSYNTHETIC ROUTE
Under hypoxia or UV stress (skin, gonads) some cholesterol is made via the MEP pathway (2C-methyl-D-erythritol 4-phosphate) with cycloartenol as intermediate.
- Major pathway for sterols in plants.
- Only a few bacteria can synthesise cholesterol de novo.

## SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS
- Cholesterol is synthesised in all nucleated cells.
- MVA pathway converts acetyl-CoA → MVA → isoprenes → squalene → lanosterol → cholesterol.
- Two major late pathways: Bloch and Kandutsch-Russell.
- Highly regulated to maintain cholesterol homeostasis.

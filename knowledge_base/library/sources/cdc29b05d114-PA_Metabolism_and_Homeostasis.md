# PHOSPHATIDIC ACID (PA) METABOLISM & HOMEOSTASIS

## (untitled panel)
Phosphatidic acid (PA) derived from 1-acyl-sn-glycerol-3-phosphate is a primary precursor for biosynthesis of other glycerolipids, although alternative pathways may be more important for generating this lipid as a signalling mediator. Dynamic changes of intracellular distribution occur via vesicular transport or at membrane contact sites by lipid transfer proteins.

## KEY FEATURES
- Central intermediate in lipid metabolism and signalling
- Very low steady-state concentration (often picomolar)
- Generated in multiple cellular membranes
- Utilized for synthesis of TAG, glycerophospholipids and other complex lipids
- Bound by specific proteins that regulate its fate

## STRUCTURE
Phosphatidic acid (1,2-diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphate)

Anionic lipid (net −1 charge at physiological pH) with a phosphomonoester head group.

### B. Other PA-binding proteins regulating fate
Proteins that bind PA and influence its use

- mTOR: Growth signalling
- Raf-1: MAPK signalling
- PKC: Kinase activation
- Opi1: CTP:phosphocholine cytidylyltransferase; PC synthesis
- Lipid droplet proteins (e.g., perilipin): Lipid droplet biogenesis
- Phosphatases (e.g., lipin): PA degradation → DG

## 5. CONTROL OF PA CONCENTRATION

### Enzymes that consume PA
- Lipins (phosphatidate phosphatases) PA → DG + Pi
- CDP-diacylglycerol synthase PA + CTP → CDP-DAG + PPi
- Diacylglycerol kinases (DGKs) PA → Phosphatidylinositol phosphate (PIP) via DAG
- Phospholipases (PLA1/2, PLD, PLC)

### Enzymes that generate PA
- GPATs + LPLATs (glycerol-3-P pathway)
- Phospholipase D (PLD)
- Diacylglycerol kinases (DGKs)
- Phospholipase C + DGK
- Phospholipase A2 on lysophospholipids

Balanced activities of enzymes and localization maintain PA in a tight range.

## KEY POINTS
- PA is a central hub in lipid metabolism and signalling.
- Multiple sources, transport routes and binding proteins create functional specificity.
- Tight regulation of PA levels is essential; pools are dynamic and context dependent.
- Homeostatic mechanisms differ among organisms according to their lipid requirements.

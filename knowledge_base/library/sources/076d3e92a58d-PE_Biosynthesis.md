# PHOSPHATIDYLETHANOLAMINE (PE) – BIOSYNTHESIS

## (untitled panel)
Two major pathways in mammalian cells: CDP-ethanolamine (Kennedy) pathway and phosphatidylserine decarboxylase pathway

## 1. CDP-ETHANOLAMINE (KENNEDY) PATHWAY – de novo
Diagram labels: Ethanolamine (dietary); Ethanolamine kinase; ATP; ADP; Ethanolamine phosphate; CTP:phosphoethanolamine cytidylyltransferase (rate-limiting); CTP; PPi; CDP-ethanolamine; Cytidine; EPT1 (Golgi) CEPT1 (ER); Diacylglycerol (DAG); Phosphatidylethanolamine (PE)

## Key points
- Occurs mainly in ER (CEPT1) and Golgi (EPT1).
- EPT1 favors plasmalogens (1-alkenyl-2-acyl PE) with PUFA.
- CEPT1 yields species with shorter-chain FA.

## Diacylglycerol (DAG) source
- From phosphatidic acid by phosphatidic acid phosphohydrolase (PAP/lipin)

Diagram labels: PA → DAG; PAP (lipin)

## Regulation
Ethanolamine phosphate phospholyase (– reversible; regulatory)

## 3. CONTRIBUTION OF PATHWAYS IN DIFFERENT CELLS
| Cell/Tissue | % PE from CDP-ethanolamine pathway | % PE from PS decarboxylase pathway |
|---|---|---|
| Cells in culture (mammalian) | <20% | >80% |
| Hamster heart, rat hepatocytes | ~95% | ~5% |
| Yeasts (e.g., S. cerevisiae) | ~30% | ~70% (PSD1) |
| Mitochondria (mammalian) | <10% | >90% |
| E. coli (bacteria) | 0% | 100% (only source) |

## (untitled panel)
Pathways are: spatially segregated, make distinct pools of PE with different fatty acid compositions, and both are essential for life.

## 2. PHOSPHATIDYLSERINE DECARBOXYLASE PATHWAY – in mitochondria
Diagram labels: Phosphatidylserine (PS) formed in ER; Transport to mitochondria (MAM contact site); Decarboxylation in mitochondria (external face of inner membrane); Phosphatidylethanolamine (PE); ER; MAM; PSD1 (Phosphatidylserine decarboxylase); CO2; MAM; Mitochondria

## Mitochondrial import of PS
- Tethers: VAPB–PTPIP51, ORPS/8, etc.
- Lipid transfer proteins: ORP family, certain OSBP-related proteins
- PS crosses cytosol and intermembrane space to reach inner membrane.

## (untitled panel)
- Most PE in mitochondria is made by this pathway.
- Conserved from bacteria to humans.
- Isoforms: PSD1 (mitochondria), PSD2 (yeast endosomal membranes).

## 5. REMODELLING OF MOLECULAR SPECIES – LANDS’ CYCLE
Diagram labels: PE (diacyl); PLA2; Lysophosphatidylethanolamine (Lyso-PE); Acyl-CoA acyltransferases; PE (remodelled)

## Key acyltransferases
- LPLAT1, 2, 3 (also act on Lyso-PE)
- LPLAT1: prefers oleoyl-CoA (18:1)
- TLCD1: Lyso-PE acyltransferase (evolutionarily conserved)

## Other sources of fatty acids
- From phosphatidylcholine (PC) e.g., arachidonic acid (20:4)
- Tissue-specific isoforms

## Lysosomal recycling
Lyso-PE/ Lyso-PC exported by Spns1 transporter for recycling.

## 7. KEY TAKE-HOME MESSAGES
- Two major pathways: CDP-ethanolamine (de novo) in ER/Golgi and PS decarboxylation in mitochondria.
- PE is synthesized in different cellular locations and released to distinct membrane pools.
- Pathways produce PE with different fatty acid patterns (ER pathway: mainly mono/di-unsaturated; PSD pathway: PUFA at sn-2).
- Remodelling by the Lands’ cycle fine-tunes molecular species.
- All routes are tightly regulated; disturbances cause serious developmental and metabolic consequences.

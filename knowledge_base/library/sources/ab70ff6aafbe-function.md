# 3. FUNCTION OF MONOGLYCOSYLCERAMIDES

## A. GALACTOSYLCERAMIDES (GalCer)
### High melting point and ordered structure
GalCer has a transition temperature well above physiological temperature. Each molecule can form up to eight inter- or intramolecular hydrogen bonds with neighbouring molecules, creating a dense network that produces a compact, para-crystalline arrangement in membranes.

### Localization in membrane rafts
GalCer is concentrated in the outer leaflet of the plasma membrane together with cholesterol and sphingomyelin in lipid rafts. The combination of hydrogen bonding and hydrophobic interactions promotes tight packing and allows binding of a wide range of raft-associated proteins, including enzymes and receptors.

### Essential for myelin structure and function
GalCer is vital for oligodendrocyte differentiation, proper axo-glial interactions and normal myelin formation.

### 2′-Hydroxy fatty acids for stability
Species containing 2′-hydroxy fatty acids are not required for myelin assembly, but are critical for long-term stability by strengthening hydrogen bonding and stabilizing membrane phase structure.

### Role in glycosynapse and myelin stability
GalCer and sulfatide on opposing myelin membranes interact via carbohydrate–carbohydrate binding to form a “glycosynapse”, which contributes to the long-term stability of the myelin sheath.

## B. GLUCOSYLCERAMIDES (GlcCer)
### Similar physical properties and raft localization
GlcCer has similar high melting point and ordered packing to GalCer and is enriched in lipid rafts in the outer leaflet of the plasma membrane.

### Precursor of complex glycosphingolipids
GlcCer is the primary precursor for most complex oligoglycosphingolipids in animals, especially in brain, where they are essential for the production of neuronal glycolipids. GlcCer itself is required for axonal growth.

### Essential in skin
Major component of epidermal lipids; necessary for lamellar body formation in the stratum corneum and for the water permeability barrier.

### Source of epidermal ceramides
Epidermal GlcCer (with sphingomyelin) is the source of unique complex ceramides found in the stratum corneum, including those with terminal hydroxyl groups and estolide-linked fatty acids. Some GlcCer is covalently linked to proteins via fatty acid hydroxyls, helping to strengthen the barrier.

GlcCer is essential for lamellar body secretion and maintenance of the water permeability barrier.

### Functions beyond structure
- Required for intracellular membrane transport, cell proliferation and survival, and for immune function.
- Disruption of global GlcCer synthase gene in mice is embryonic lethal. Crucial for survival of cancer cells; over-expression promotes tumor progression and drug resistance.
- In C. elegans, GlcCer with 22:0 fatty acid and iso-methyl-branched base mediates glucose response and acts as a longevity metabolite via clathrin localization.
- In plants, GlcCer is critical for cell differentiation, organogenesis, cellulose synthesis, and stress tolerance (cold, drought).
- In fungi, GlcCer is essential for cell wall assembly, cell division, differentiation and signaling; methyl branch in long-chain base is required for division and alkali-tolerance and influences virulence.

## C. GLUCOSYLCERAMIDES IN PLANTS
Scottish thistle (Onopordum acanthium)

- Synthesized in the ER by conserved GCS1 using UDP-glucose and ceramide.
- Localized in lipid rafts with sterols in the plasma membrane and associated with specific proteins.
- Necessary for cellulose synthesis and cell differentiation.
- Help plasma membrane withstand cold and drought stresses. Species with 2-hydroxy monounsaturated very-long-chain fatty acids and 4-cis double bonds in bases are enriched in tolerant plants.
- Only small amounts are used as precursors of oligoglycosylceramides.
- Some plant GlcCer (e.g., with Δ9 double bond in LCB) induce fruiting in the fungus Schizophyllum commune.

## D. GLUCOSYLCERAMIDES IN FUNGI
- Major components of plasma membrane and cell wall.
- Involved in cell wall assembly, cell division, differentiation and signaling.
- Methyl branch in long-chain base is required for cell division, alkali-tolerance and is recognized by host immune system; exported in extracellular vesicles, influencing virulence.
- Not precursors for oligoglycosylceramides in fungi.
- Some fungal GlcCer (with Δ9-methyl in the base or double bonds at Δ4 and/or Δ8) trigger plant defence responses.

In summary: Galactosylceramides are essential for myelin structure and stability through ordered raft domains and glycosynapse formation with sulfatide. Glucosylceramides are versatile lipids required for membrane organization, barrier function, glycosphingolipid biosynthesis, development, stress responses and signaling in animals, plants and fungi.

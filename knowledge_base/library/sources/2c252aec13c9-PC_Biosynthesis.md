# 2. PHOSPHATIDYLCHOLINE (PC) – BIOSYNTHESIS

## A. CHOLINE METABOLISM AND UPTAKE
Dietary sources
- Animal-based foods
- Cruciferous vegetables
- Soybeans
- Degradation of choline-containing lipids

Functions of choline
- Phospholipid (PC) synthesis
- Acetylcholine (neurotransmitter)
- Methyl group donor (via betaine)
- High demand in pregnancy

Diagram labels: Choline; FLVCR1/2 Transporters

## 1 Choline kinase (ChoK)
Diagram labels: Choline; ATP; ADP; Phosphocholine

## 2 CTP:phosphocholine cytidylyltransferase (CCT)
Diagram labels: Phosphocholine; CTP; PPi; CDP-choline; Cytidine

- Two isoforms: CCTα (nucleoplasm → cytoplasm) and CCTβ (cytoplasm).
- Activated by reversible binding to ER or nuclear membranes.
- Rate-limiting step; feedback-regulated by PC levels.
- ChoKα also has regulatory functions.

## B. KENNEDY (CDP-CHOLINE) PATHWAY – MAIN ROUTE
## 3 CDP-choline:1,2-diacylglycerol choline/ethanolamine-phosphotransferase (CEPT1) – ER and nuclei
Diagram labels: Cytidine; sn-1,2-Diacylglycerol (DAG); Phosphatidylcholine (PC)

- CEPT1: major enzyme in ER (dimer; 10 transmembrane segments per protomer); interior hydrophobic chamber binds acyl chains).
- Related CPT1 in trans-Golgi prefers 1-alkyl diacylglycerols → PC species with ether linkages.

## (untitled panel)
The Kennedy pathway is distinct from the CDP-diacylglycerol pathway used for PG, PI and cardiolipin.

## Historical note
The Kennedy pathway was discovered serendipitously when ATP preparations contained CTP as an impurity. Kennedy and co-workers showed the impurity was essential for PC formation.

## C. N-METHYLTRANSFERASE PATHWAY (Sequential methylation of PE)
Diagram labels: Phosphatidylethanolamine (PE); PE N-methyltransferase (PEMT, SAM-dependent); SAM; SAH; Monomethyl-phosphatidylethanolamine (MMPE); PE N-methyltransferase (SAM-dependent); SAM; SAH; Dimethyl-phosphatidylethanolamine (DMPE); PE N-methyltransferase (SAM-dependent); SAM → SAH; Phosphatidylcholine (PC)

## (untitled panel)
- Single enzyme (~20 kDa) in two isoforms in hepatocytes: PEMT1 (ER, major) and PEMT2 (mitochondria).
- Major pathway in liver when dietary choline is low.
- Important in yeast and many bacteria.
- Uses S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) as methyl donor.

## D. CDP-DIACYLGLYCEROL PATHWAY (in some bacteria)
Diagram labels: Phosphatidic acid (PA); CTP; CDP-diacylglycerol; Cytidine; Phosphocholine; Phosphatidylcholine (PC)

## (untitled panel)
Utilized by several Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria; analogous to pathway for PG, PI and cardiolipin.

## E. REMODELLING OF MOLECULAR SPECIES (LANDS’ CYCLE)
Diagram labels: PC (diacyl form); Phospholipase A2 (specific for sn-2); H2O; Lysophosphatidylcholine (LPC); Lysophosphatidylcholine acyltransferase (LPCAT) (acyl-CoA); PC (new acyl in sn-2)

## (untitled panel)
- Maintains fatty acid composition and membrane properties.
- Multiple LPCAT isoforms with different acyl-CoA specificities and tissue distributions.
- Also participates in incorporation of polyunsaturated fatty acids into PC.

## F. REGULATION AND TRANSPORT
- CCTα is the key regulatory point (feedback inhibited by PC).
- Lipins are regulated by phosphorylation and interact with membranes dynamically.
- PC is transported from ER to other membranes by vesicular and non-vesicular (lipid transfer proteins) routes.

Diagram labels: ER; PC; Golgi; Plasma membrane

## Key points
- Two major routes in eukaryotes: Kennedy (primary) and N-methyltransferase.
- CDP-diacylglycerol pathway in certain bacteria.
- DAG precursor from PA via lipin phosphatases.
- Remodelling (Lands’ cycle) finalizes PC molecular species.

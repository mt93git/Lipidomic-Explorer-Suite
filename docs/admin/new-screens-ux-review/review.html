Storage

At the freezer with a phone, Paula finds an aliquot, opens a box, places what is waiting, and moves or removes with a reason. The wording change is X3.

What works
  • The URL keeps what is open (?q=, ?box=, ?act=&aliquot=), so Back, reload and shared links work.
  • A refused place (position A1 already holds …) keeps Box and Position and moves the focus to the refusal (measured on the phone: alert on screen, focus on it).
  • After Place, Move or Remove the page reloads at the top and the banner is on screen at 390 (top 496 px). Move and Remove say the old position stays in the history.
  • A form that no longer applies (s12, Place for an aliquot already in a box) says so and points to Move instead of failing.
  • Search ignores case (ord046-s002 finds it); "nothing found" and "every aliquot is in a box" each have their own sentence.

QC review

After ingest, Paula opens the order, excludes the samples that failed with a reason, optionally marks it QC reviewed, then builds the dataset in Release. How Paula gets here from Ingest results is X2.

What works
  • The list shows only Processed and QC reviewed orders, with stage, results file and exclusion count. Its empty state points to Ingest results.
  • An exclusion needs a reason (a blank one is refused in the row). Withdrawing needs one too, and the decision stays in the audit log.
  • Mark QC reviewed sits behind a confirm that says the stage does not move back. The step is labelled optional, and Release builds from Processed as well.
  • Once the dataset is built, the screen reads only and says where the exclusions went (q08). An unknown order code gets its own notice (q09).
  • Clients never reach it: /qc-review as a client lands on Client home.

How it was made

cd frontends/admin
NEXT_PUBLIC_NEXO_URL=http://127.0.0.1:3120/nexo NEXT_PUBLIC_NEXO_CLIENT_ACCESS=1 npm run dev -- --port 3110
# then, also from frontends/admin
node ../../docs/admin/new-screens-ux-review/capture.mjs              # before  -> screenshots/
PASS=after node ../../docs/admin/new-screens-ux-review/capture.mjs   # after   -> after/
# BASE=<origin>/admin for another port
  • screenshots/ was captured with the app source at b386617a (admin/phase1) and after/ with this branch. Both come from the same walk with the same synthetic data, at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, full page.
  • The script prints the measurements quoted here: sideways overflow of the page, frames that scroll sideways with the column headers they hide or cut, controls under 36 px and under 24 px tall on the phone, text under 4.5:1 against its painted background, and where the page and focus are after a tap.
  • The demo state lives in the browser cookie, so each pass starts clean and scene order matters. The walk ends by archiving every panel and instrument and by emptying the boxes, for the empty states.
  • Each image here shows a slice of the capture. Click it to open the whole capture.
  • Not captured: the read-only Instruments & panels view (checked by unit test) and a sample page with aliquots (checked by reading code).
